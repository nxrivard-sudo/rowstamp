Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-06 · RowStamp is operated by The Picnic Shoppe LLC (dba The Picnic Collective) · nxrivard@gmail.com

Summary: RowStamp works only with the spreadsheet you open it in and the Google Drive files it creates or that you choose. Your data stays in your Google account and is never sent to us.

1. Permissions the add-on requests. RowStamp requests two Google permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly: view and manage the spreadsheet in which you run RowStamp, and no other spreadsheet.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: see, edit and create only the specific Google Drive files that RowStamp creates, or that you open with RowStamp. RowStamp cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

The Google Workspace Marketplace may also show that the add-on can see your email address and basic profile information. Google adds those permissions to every Marketplace listing. RowStamp's code does not read, store or transmit them.

2. What the add-on reads. When you use it in a spreadsheet, RowStamp reads the header row and the cell values of the tab that is open, so it can match columns and fill the rows you choose. It reads the text of the template document you select: a template RowStamp created for you, or a Google Doc you chose by clicking Allow access to this document in the RowStamp panel in Google Docs.

3. What the add-on creates and changes. In your Google Drive, RowStamp creates a folder named "RowStamp" (holding the templates it creates) and a folder inside it named "RowStamp output" (holding the documents it fills). In the open spreadsheet, it adds "RowStamp doc" and "RowStamp PDF" link columns to the right of your data. If you ask for sample data, it adds a new tab with made-up rows. It does not change or delete your existing cells or files. Everything it creates belongs to you and stays in your account until you delete it.

4. PDF links. A "RowStamp PDF" link is a Google Docs export link. When you open it, your browser downloads the PDF directly from Google. RowStamp doesn't receive the file.

5. What is stored, and where. RowStamp keeps a few settings in your own Google account's add-on storage (Apps Script user properties): your license key if you buy Pro; the IDs and names of your templates; the IDs of the two RowStamp folders; your column choices for each spreadsheet tab and template (stored as spreadsheet, tab and template IDs plus column numbers, not cell contents); and a count of documents created today. These settings are used only to run the add-on.

6. What we collect. Nothing from your spreadsheets or documents. The add-on makes no network requests to us or to any third party, and no cell values, document text or file names are sent anywhere. Processing happens inside Google's Apps Script environment, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. License keys are checked offline inside the add-on.

7. Purchases. When you buy Pro, the payment processor (Stripe) collects your payment details and the email you provide. We receive your email, the plan and the amount so we can send your license key and handle refunds. We never see your full card number.

8. Website. This website uses no cookies and no analytics scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

9. Google user data. RowStamp's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train AI models, and do not transfer it to anyone.

10. Removing the add-on and your data. To stop RowStamp's access, uninstall it (in Google Sheets, open the add-ons side panel or Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons) and remove it from your Google account's third-party access page. That also ends its access to the files it created. The documents and folders it created stay in your Drive until you delete them. Purchase records can be deleted on request, subject to tax record-keeping law: nxrivard@gmail.com.

11. Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
