RowStamp

Make a certificate or invoice for each row of a Google Sheets™ spreadsheet, as a Google Doc with a PDF download link.

RowStamp is a spreadsheet add-on. Pick a template, choose the rows, and click Fill documents. Each row becomes its own Google Doc, and the links are added in new columns next to your data. Your existing cells are never changed.

  1. Template. Start from the built-in certificate or invoice template (RowStamp creates it as a Google Doc you can edit and brand), or use one of your own Docs.
  2. Fields. Placeholders like {{Name}} are matched to your column headers automatically. Change any match under Check fields.
  3. Rows. Fill all rows, a range like 2-20, or the rows you selected.
  4. Links. Each row gets a "RowStamp doc" link and, if you want, a "RowStamp PDF" download link. Rows that already have a document are skipped next time, so you can add rows and run it again.

Good for

  • Certificates: course completion, training, attendance, awards.
  • Invoices and receipts: one invoice per order or client row.
  • Letters: offer letters, confirmations, any Doc with a few personalized fields.

Private by design

RowStamp runs inside Google's Apps Script. It asks for only two permissions: the spreadsheet you open it in, and the Google Drive files it creates or that you choose. It can't see the rest of your Drive. It makes no requests to outside servers and sends no data to us or anyone else. See the privacy policy.
